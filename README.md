## 🚀 Featured Projects

### [AEVON — Academic Intelligence Platform](https://github.com/rishabhnayakk/AEVON)
A full-stack EdTech platform designed to help students, teachers, and administrators make better use of academic data.

- Student performance dashboards, exam analytics, and leaderboards.
- AI-powered performance insights, at-risk student detection, and personalized study recommendations.
- Teacher workflows for marks, notifications, and study materials.
- **Built with:** Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, JavaScript, Groq AI.

### [ZeroTrader — Live Trading Terminal](https://github.com/rishabhnayakk/ZeroTrader)
An institutional-style market analysis and trading workspace built around live market data.

- Market tracker, sector heatmaps, historical OHLC analysis, and candlestick charts.
- Option-chain and Greeks analysis, market news intelligence, and portfolio/order workflows.
- **Built with:** React, FastAPI, Vite, Zerodha Kite Connect.

### [Syllabus Builder](https://github.com/rishabhnayakk/Syllabus-Builder)
A lightweight study-planning application for organizing a syllabus and tracking learning progress.

- Manage subjects and topics with CRUD workflows.
- Track topic completion and retain progress with browser LocalStorage.
- **Built with:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript.

### [Smart Restaurant Management System](https://github.com/rishabhnayakk/smart-restaurant)
A C++ console application for handling restaurant orders and daily operations.

- Order creation and status tracking, revenue calculations, and daily summaries.
- Uses a custom doubly linked list and file-based persistence.
- **Built with:** C++17, OOP, Data Structures, File Handling.

### [LeetCode Solutions](https://github.com/rishabhnayakk/leetcode-solutions)
My ongoing C++ problem-solving log as I strengthen DSA fundamentals and analytical thinking.
